# Roadmap

The quarterly roadmap is maintained in the lab's private repository
(`hypneum-lab/library`, `roadmap/ROADMAP.md`) since 2026-11-15. It is an
operational planning document (hour budget, hosts, dated decisions,
incidents); scientific records stay in the study repositories.

What stays public here:

- the six programme principles, `docs/PROJECT-MANAGEMENT.md` §0 (the
  public source for "principle 3", "principle 6" cited by pre-registrations,
  ADRs and amendments);
- the hypothesis catalogue and its deltas, `docs/research/` (status of
  each claim, errata cited);
- the organisation profile, `profile/README.md`;
- the project-management rules, `docs/PROJECT-MANAGEMENT.md`.
